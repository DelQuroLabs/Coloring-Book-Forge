⚠️ Heads up: DALL-E 3 forging of 30–80 pages can consume $5–$20 of OpenAI credits and uses significant storage. Run it from your production server, not this sandbox.

What you get:

  • book_manifest.json — every page with its prompt, filename, and KDP placement
  • book_prompts.csv — spreadsheet for batch forging / tracking
  • kdp_preview.html — printable book preview with KDP trim/bleed guides
  • forge_runner.py — drop-and-run script that forges every image via DALL-E 3 on your production machine and saves them into output_production/<book>/
  • Cover & back-cover prompts prepared with correct trim/bleed dimensions

🚀 Publishing Checklist (per book)

  1. ✅ Click "Auto-Plan" → title/subtitle/blurb/keywords generated
  2. 📦 Click "Download Book Package" → get manifest + runner + preview
  3. ⚡ On production server: python3 forge_runner.py book_manifest.json (uses DALL-E 3)

  5. 📐 Open kdp_preview.html → Print-to-PDF with correct trim/bleed settings
